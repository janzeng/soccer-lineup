How the Generator Works

Fairness First: The engine prioritizes equal playing time above all else. It strictly calculates bench rotations to ensure shifts are distributed as evenly as mathematically possible.

Positional Preferences: Players are assigned to their preferred roles whenever possible. However, if preferences conflict with fair playing time, the engine will override preferences to force a fair substitution.

Goalie Rules: Goalie shifts are mandatory. If multiple players select Goalie, they share the net and their field/bench time is balanced around those shifts. If no one selects Goalie, the entire team takes turns in the net.

No Subs Lock: If you have exactly the minimum required players for a format, the engine will automatically lock the field positions for the entire half to prevent unnecessary shuffling.
