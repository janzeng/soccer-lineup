Coach's Guide

  • The Fair-Play Algorithm: The generator tracks every shift. When subbing, it prioritizes putting in players who have sat on the bench the longest, and pulling out players who have played the most consecutive shifts.
  • Position Locks: A player will only be substituted into positions you have checked for them. Ensure you check enough boxes across your roster to fill the field!
  • Goalie Logic:
    • 1 Goalie: Plays the entire game.
    • 2 Goalies: Swaps at halftime. The 2nd half Goalie gets Q2S2 completely off to gear up!
    • 3+ Goalies: Rotates evenly every quarter.
  • Absences: Checking 'Abs' completely removes a player from the math for this game, but remembers their positions for next week.
  • Portability: Use Export/Import to move your roster from your laptop to your phone for game day!
